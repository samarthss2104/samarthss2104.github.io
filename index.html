About

I am a 16-year-old flautist from Bengaluru, learning and performing in both great traditions of Indian classical music: the Carnatic music of South India on the Venu, and the Hindustani music of North India on the Bansuri. I train on the flute under Pandit Shri Prakash Hegde (Hindustani) and Vidwan Ravichandra Kulur (Carnatic). My first guru in music and rhythm was Shri Swapnil Sapre, and training in tabla and mridangam gives my melodic playing a strong rhythmic foundation. I perform as a soloist, as an accompanist, and as a member of a fusion band.

Guru–shishya

Learning from the masters

In Indian classical music, knowledge passes from guru to shishya, student, through years of close listening, imitation and guidance. I am fortunate to learn from two accomplished flautists from Bengaluru, one in each tradition, and from my gurus in rhythm.
