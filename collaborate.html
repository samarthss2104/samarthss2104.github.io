Beyond the classical stage

Collaboration & exploration

Fusion band

As part of a band, I bring the bansuri into fusion and contemporary settings alongside drums and other instruments. Band work has taught me to adapt my sound, blend with new textures, and improvise within unfamiliar musical structures.

Flute ensembles

Sharing the stage with fellow flautists sharpens my listening, intonation and sense of ensemble, as every phrase has to breathe together.

Reimagining songs

On Instagram and YouTube I reimagine songs from around the world and across eras on the flute, from pop to Bollywood, with new reels every few weeks. Learning Western music theory gives me a new vocabulary for harmony and arrangement. I also write Indian-style notation for songs and welcome collaborations.
